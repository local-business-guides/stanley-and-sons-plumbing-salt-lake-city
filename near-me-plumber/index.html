What to prepare and expect

Keep these details close before you make contact:

  • Your full Salt Lake City service address, including any unit number.
  • The fixture, room, or area where you noticed the problem.
  • When it started and whether it has changed since then.
  • Any steps you have already taken, such as turning off a fixture.
  • Questions about access, timing, and what the plumber needs to assess it.

A common mistake is to leave out small details because they seem unrelated. A repeated sound, a damp area, or a change in how a fixture works may help explain the concern, so mention what you have observed without guessing at the cause.
